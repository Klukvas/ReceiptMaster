# Turn Your Customer List into a CRM

A customer list is just names and phone numbers. A CRM tells you who your best customers are, what they buy, and when they last ordered. receiptmaster bridges this gap automatically.

## Automatic Tracking

Every time you create an order for a recipient, receiptmaster updates their profile:

- **Total orders** — How many times they've ordered
- **Total spend** — How much they've spent across all orders
- **Last order date** — When they last purchased

You don't need to calculate this manually — it's always up to date.

## Smart Badges

Recipients earn badges based on their activity:

- **Top Customer** — Spent over a threshold amount
- **Frequent Buyer** — Placed more than 10 orders

These badges appear in the recipient list, making it easy to identify your most valuable customers at a glance.

## The Detail Drawer

Click any recipient to open the detail drawer. Here you see:

- Full contact information
- Complete order history (with status and amounts)
- Running total spend
- Quick actions: edit, create new order, view orders

This gives you a 360-degree view of each customer without leaving the recipients page.

## Finding Your Best Customers

Sort the recipients table by **Total Spent** to see your highest-value customers. Sort by **Orders** to find your most frequent buyers. These two lists often overlap, but not always — a customer who places many small orders is different from one who places few large ones.

## Practical Use Cases

**Loyalty rewards** — Use the "Top Customer" badge to identify customers who deserve a discount or special offer.

**Re-engagement** — Sort by last order date to find customers who haven't ordered recently. A quick message can bring them back.

**Upselling** — Check a customer's order history before they visit. If they always buy Product A, suggest Product B that pairs with it.

## From List to Strategy

The data is already there — receiptmaster collects it automatically. The shift from "customer list" to "CRM" isn't about adding features. It's about looking at the data you already have and using it to make better decisions.
