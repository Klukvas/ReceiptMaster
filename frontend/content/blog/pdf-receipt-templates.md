# 15 Professional Receipt Templates for Every Business

First impressions matter, even on a receipt. receiptmaster ships with 15 professionally designed templates across four categories, so you can match your brand voice perfectly.

## Business Templates

**Standard** — The default choice. Clean lines, professional layout, and just enough branding to look polished. Perfect for everyday invoicing.

**Classic** — A formal, structured invoice with uppercase headings and a monochrome palette. Ideal for B2B transactions.

**Corporate** — Pure black-and-white. No color, no decoration — just data. Built for enterprises that need maximum readability.

**Delivery Note** — Shows quantities without prices. Designed for warehouse and logistics teams who need packing slips.

## Creative Templates

**Modern** — Card-based layout with colored metadata boxes and optional QR code. Great for tech companies and startups.

**Dark** — Dark navy background with cyan accents. Sophisticated and eye-catching for premium brands.

**Vintage** — Old-paper aesthetic with serif typography and decorative ornaments. Ideal for craft businesses and boutiques.

**Wave** — Soft, friendly design with wave-shaped graphics. Perfect for retail or customer-facing receipts.

**Tech** — Bold geometric shapes with red and blue accents. High-energy design for tech retailers.

## Minimal Templates

**Minimal** — Maximum whitespace, grayscale rendering. Designed for archival purposes.

**Compact** — Space-efficient layout with tight spacing. Uses less paper and ink for high-volume businesses.

**Thermal** — 80mm format specifically designed for POS thermal printers with monospace font.

## Premium Templates

**Elegant** — Refined typography with thin lines and centered layout. The luxury option for high-end retail.

**Branded** — Fully customizable with your brand color as the primary accent. Makes the template uniquely yours.

**Proforma Invoice** — A formal pre-invoice with a PROFORMA watermark, banking details section, and payment terms.

## Every Template Supports

- **3 languages**: English, Ukrainian, Russian
- **Custom branding**: Your logo, company name, and contact info
- **SHA256 verification**: Every receipt is cryptographically signed
- **PDF generation**: Background processing via queue — no page blocking

Head to **Settings → Receipt Design** to preview all templates with your own data.
