# How to Generate Receipts in Multiple Languages

If your business serves customers across Ukraine and the EU, you need receipts in their language. receiptmaster supports three languages natively: English, Ukrainian, and Russian.

## Setting the Default Language

Go to **Settings → Language** and choose your default receipt language. All new receipts will use this language unless overridden per order.

## Per-Receipt Language Override

When generating a receipt, you can select a different language for that specific document. This is useful when you have a mixed customer base — Ukrainian customers get receipts in Ukrainian, while international clients receive English invoices.

## What Gets Translated?

- All template labels: "Invoice", "Bill To", "Subtotal", "Total", "Date", etc.
- Currency formatting adjusts to locale conventions
- Date formatting follows the selected language
- Footer text and notes

## What Stays the Same?

- Product names (as entered in your catalog)
- Customer names and addresses
- Your company information
- Prices and quantities

## Template Compatibility

All 15 templates support all 3 languages. The layout automatically adjusts for longer Ukrainian and Russian text — templates use flexible widths rather than fixed columns.

## Best Practice

Set your primary language as the default, and use per-receipt overrides for international customers. This way, 90% of your receipts generate automatically in the right language, and you only intervene for exceptions.
