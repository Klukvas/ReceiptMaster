# Understanding Your Revenue: Analytics and CSV Export

Numbers tell stories. receiptmaster's analytics dashboard helps you read yours — revenue trends, top products, top customers, and order status breakdowns, all filterable by date range.

## Revenue vs Turnover

These are different metrics:

- **Revenue** = Sale price minus purchase price (your profit)
- **Turnover** = Total sale amount (your gross sales)

receiptmaster tracks both independently. You can switch between Revenue and Turnover views in the analytics dashboard using the segmented control at the top.

## Date Range Presets

Quick filters let you view the last 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or a full year. For custom ranges, use the date picker to set exact start and end dates.

## Revenue Over Time

A sparkline chart on the dashboard shows daily revenue or turnover over your selected period. This helps you spot trends: are weekends stronger than weekdays? Is revenue growing month over month?

## By Product and By Customer

Two breakdown views show you:

- **Revenue by product** — Which items generate the most profit?
- **Revenue by customer** — Who are your most valuable clients?

These help you make strategic decisions: stock more of what sells, and nurture your best customers.

## Order Status Summary

A donut chart breaks down your orders by status: confirmed, paid, draft, cancelled. A high ratio of drafts to confirmed orders might indicate friction in your checkout process.

## CSV Export

Every analytics view has an **Export CSV** button. Click it to download the current filtered data as a spreadsheet. Send it to your accountant, import it into Excel, or archive it for tax season.

## Pro Tip: Weekly Review

Set aside 10 minutes every Monday to review your dashboard. Look at last week's revenue, check the low stock widget, and glance at the order status breakdown. This single habit catches problems before they become crises.
