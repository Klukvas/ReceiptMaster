# Getting Started with receiptmaster

Welcome to receiptmaster — the complete back-office platform for modern merchants. This guide walks you through setting up your account and generating your first professional receipt in under 5 minutes.

## Step 1: Create Your Account

Head to [receiptmaster.org](https://receiptmaster.org) and click **Start for free**. You'll need just an email and password — no credit card required. The Free plan gives you up to 10 products and 20 orders per month.

## Step 2: Add Your Products

Navigate to **Products** in the sidebar. Click **Create Product** and fill in:

- **Name** — your product or service name
- **Purchase price** — what you pay (for margin tracking)
- **Sale price** — what your customer pays
- **Quantity** — current stock level

receiptmaster automatically calculates your margin percentage and displays color-coded badges: green for healthy margins, yellow for thin margins, and red for negative margins.

## Step 3: Add Recipients

Go to **Recipients** and add your customers. Each recipient has a name, phone number, and optional address. When you create orders for them, receiptmaster tracks their total spend and order history automatically.

## Step 4: Create Your First Order

Click **Orders → Create Order**. Select a recipient, add line items from your product catalog, and set quantities. The order starts in **Draft** status. Review the total, then click **Confirm** to move it to **Confirmed**.

## Step 5: Generate a Receipt

Once an order is confirmed, click the receipt icon in the order row. Choose from 15 professional templates, select your language (English, Ukrainian, or Russian), and click **Generate**. receiptmaster creates a PDF in the background — no waiting.

Your receipt includes:

- Your company branding and logo
- Line items with quantities and prices
- Subtotal, taxes, and grand total
- A **SHA256 integrity hash** for verification
- A unique shareable link

## What's Next?

- **Customize your receipts** — Go to Settings → Receipt Design to choose a template, upload your logo, and set your brand color
- **Track your revenue** — The Analytics dashboard shows revenue and turnover charts, filterable by date range
- **Go Pro** — Upgrade to Pro for up to 100 products, 500 orders/month, and all 15 templates
