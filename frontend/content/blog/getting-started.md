# Getting Started with receiptmaster

Welcome to receiptmaster — the complete back-office platform for modern merchants. This guide walks you through setting up your account and issuing your first professional document in about 10 minutes.

## Step 1: Create Your Account

Head to [receiptmaster.org](https://receiptmaster.org) and click **Start for free**. You'll need just an email and password — no credit card required. The Free plan gives you up to 10 products and 20 orders per month.

## Step 2: Fill In Your Company Details

Open **Settings → Documents**. Everything about your documents lives on this one page:

- **Your business** — company name, address and contacts shown at the top of every document. Open **Tax and bank details** to add your EDRPOU or RNOKPP and IBAN: invoices and acts need them.
- **Requisites for documents** — legal form, bank name, VAT status, the signatory's name and position, and your default payment term.
- **Template and title**, **Colour and logo** — how your documents look. A live preview next to the form updates as you type.
- **Document numbering** — a prefix, the number of digits and whether the year is part of the number, separately for each document type. You can also restart the count every year.
- **Signature and stamp** — upload images of your signature and stamp to place them on documents.

You can skip anything you don't need yet. When a document is missing a requisite, receiptmaster tells you which one and takes you straight to the field.

## Step 3: Add Your Products

Navigate to **Products** in the sidebar. Click **Create Product** and fill in:

- **Name** — your product or service name
- **Purchase price** — what you pay (for margin tracking)
- **Sale price** — what your customer pays
- **Quantity** — current stock level

receiptmaster automatically calculates your margin percentage and displays color-coded badges: green for healthy margins, yellow for thin margins, and red for negative margins.

## Step 4: Add Recipients

Go to **Recipients** and add your customers. Each recipient has a name, phone number, and optional address. For business customers, fill in **Requisites for documents** — legal name, EDRPOU or RNOKPP, IBAN, bank and legal address — so they appear on invoices, acts, sales receipts and quotations. receiptmaster checks the tax number and IBAN as you type, and tracks each customer's total spend and order history automatically.

## Step 5: Create Your First Order

Click **Orders → Create Order**. Select a recipient, add line items from your product catalog, and set quantities. The order starts in **Draft** status. Review the total, then click **Confirm** to move it to **Confirmed**.

## Step 6: Issue Documents

Open the order's **More actions** menu or its details. The **Documents** section shows what you can create for the order:

- **Waybill** — the delivery note for the goods
- **Invoice** — a request for payment with your bank details
- **Act of Completed Work** — for services
- **Quote** — a commercial offer, available even while the order is a draft
- **Sales receipt** and **Packing list**
- **Return note** — when a customer brings goods back; you choose which lines and how many

Pick a template (receiptmaster only offers the ones that can carry the document type), add what the type needs, such as an invoice's due date, and click **Create**. The PDF is generated in the background — no waiting — and numbered according to your settings.

Want a waybill for every order without clicking? Turn on **Automatic waybills** in **Settings → Advanced**, and one is created whenever you confirm an order.

## Step 7: Share and Keep Track

Each document has **Download**, **Print** and **Share** buttons. **Share** gives you a link your customer can open without an account. If something needs fixing, **Replace** the document with a corrected version, or **Void** it with a reason.

The **Documents** page in the sidebar lists every document across all your orders, with filters by type, status and date.

receiptmaster keeps the 10 most recent documents of each type and asks before the oldest one is removed. Invoices, acts and return notes are never removed.

## What's Next?

- **Fine-tune your design** — Go to Settings → Documents to switch templates, upload your logo, set your brand color, and save favourite setups as presets
- **Track your revenue** — The Analytics dashboard shows revenue and turnover charts, filterable by date range
- **Go Pro** — Upgrade to Pro for up to 100 products, 500 orders/month, and all 15 templates
