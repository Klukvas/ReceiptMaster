# Inventory Tracking Made Simple

Spreadsheets work until they don't. Once you're managing more than 20 products, you need a system that warns you before you run out of stock and shows you which products are actually profitable.

## Color-Coded Margin Badges

Every product in receiptmaster shows a margin badge:

- **Green** (>30%) — Healthy margin. Keep selling.
- **Yellow** (10–30%) — Thin margin. Consider raising prices.
- **Red** (<10%) — Losing money or breaking even. Investigate.

The margin is calculated automatically from your purchase and sale prices. No formulas, no errors.

## Low Stock Alerts

When a product's quantity drops below a threshold, a **Low Stock** badge appears on the product card and in the dashboard widget. This gives you a visual warning before you hit zero.

The dashboard's Low Stock widget shows all products below threshold in one place — no need to scroll through your entire catalog.

## Stock Badges

Products display one of three stock states:

- **In Stock** — Quantity above threshold
- **Low Stock** — Quantity below threshold but not zero
- **Out of Stock** — Zero quantity

These badges appear in the product list, in the order creation modal (so you don't accidentally sell something you don't have), and in the dashboard overview.

## Bulk Operations

Need to delete old products? Select multiple rows and click **Bulk Delete**. Need to audit your catalog? Sort by margin percentage to find your worst performers, or sort by quantity to find items running low.

## The Dashboard View

The home dashboard shows four key metrics at a glance: total products, total recipients, total orders, and total receipts. Below that, the Low Stock widget highlights products that need attention — all without navigating away from the main screen.
