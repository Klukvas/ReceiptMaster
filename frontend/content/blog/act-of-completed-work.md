# Act of Completed Work: What to Include and How to Fill It In

An act of completed work (in Ukrainian, акт виконаних робіт or акт наданих послуг) is a short document in which the contractor and the customer confirm that specific work was done, in a specific period, for a specific amount. In Ukraine it is the usual primary document for services: a sole proprietor (ФОП) or a company issues it once the job is finished, both sides sign, and each keeps a copy for its accounting records.

To fill one in, you need four groups of information: who did the work and who accepted it, what exactly was done and when, how much it costs, and who signs on each side. Below, each field is explained, followed by a filled-in example and the mistakes that most often make an accountant send an act back.

## What an act of completed work must contain

Ukrainian accounting law lists the details a primary document has to carry. For an act, in practice that means:

| Field | What to write | Typical mistake |
|---|---|---|
| Title, number, date | Act of completed work (services rendered) No. 2026-000014 dated 31.10.2026 | Reusing a number, or dating the act before the work ended |
| Contractor | Registered name, tax code (ЄДРПОУ for a company, РНОКПП for a sole proprietor), address | A shop or brand name instead of the registered one |
| Customer | The same set of details for the customer | The customer's tax code left out |
| Contract | Number and date of the contract the work was done under | Omitted even though a contract exists |
| Period | The dates the work covers | Only the month, when the contract bills by stage |
| List of work | One line per service, with quantity, unit, price and amount | Vague lines such as "services" or "consulting" |
| Totals | Total amount, plus the VAT line if the contractor is a VAT payer | A total that does not match the lines |
| Statement | A sentence that the work is done in full and the customer has no claims | Missing, so the act does not actually record acceptance |
| Signatures | Position, full name and signature of each signatory; stamp if the party uses one | Signed by someone without authority to sign |

Two notes on this list. The exact set of mandatory details comes from Ukrainian law and from your own accounting policy, and both get amended, so check the current requirements with your accountant or the official text instead of trusting a template found online. And the stamp: most Ukrainian businesses are no longer required to use a seal, so an act without one is normal. If your company does use a stamp, or your customer asks for it, it goes next to the contractor's signature, in the space marked "М.П.".

## How to fill in an act of completed work, step by step

1. **Take the parties' details from the contract.** Copy the registered names, tax codes and addresses of both parties exactly as the contract gives them. If the customer has moved since signing, use the current registered address.
2. **Number the act.** Keep one running sequence for acts, separate from invoices. A gap or a repeated number raises questions during a tax inspection or audit.
3. **Set the date and the period.** The date of the act is the day it is drawn up, on or after the last day of the work. The period says which work it covers: 01.10.2026 to 31.10.2026 for monthly support, or the dates of a one-off job.
4. **Describe the work line by line.** Write something the customer can recognise and check. "Support of the company website, October 2026" says far more than "IT services". If the contract has stages or an estimate, reuse its wording and order.
5. **Check the money.** Quantity times price on each line, then the total. A VAT payer shows the VAT included in the total. If you are not a VAT payer, say so on the act ("без ПДВ", without VAT) so the customer's accountant does not have to guess.
6. **Add the acceptance statement.** The usual wording is that the work was done in full and on time, and the customer has no claims about its scope, quality or timing. Change it if the facts differ, for example when only part of the work is accepted and the rest moves to the next act.
7. **Sign, exchange and store.** Each side signs. On paper you make two copies, one for each party. Many businesses now exchange acts electronically, signed with a qualified electronic signature (КЕП). Either way, keep your copy with your accounting records for the period your accountant names.

## A worked example with numbers

A web developer registered as a sole proprietor, not a VAT payer, maintains a client company's website under a contract signed in January. At the end of October they issue an act for the month. The figures are illustrative.

| # | Description of work (services) | Unit | Qty | Price, UAH | Amount, UAH |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Website support, October 2026 | service | 1 | 6,000.00 | 6,000.00 |
| 2 | Landing page design for the autumn campaign | service | 1 | 9,500.00 | 9,500.00 |
| 3 | Content updates on request | hour | 8 | 450.00 | 3,600.00 |
| | **Total** | | | | **19,100.00** |

The rest of this act:

- **Number and date:** No. 2026-000014, 31.10.2026.
- **Contract:** No. 7 dated 15.01.2026.
- **Period:** 01.10.2026 to 31.10.2026.
- **VAT:** without VAT, because the contractor is not a VAT payer.
- **Statement:** the work was done in full and the customer has no claims.
- **Signatures:** the contractor on the left; on the right, the customer's director, with position and full name written out.

## Common mistakes that get an act returned

- **Dated before the work ended.** An act dated 25 October for work "in October" invites questions. Date it on or after the last day of the period.
- **Amounts that differ from the invoice.** If you invoiced 19,100 UAH and the act says 19,000, one of the two documents is wrong. Issue both from the same figures.
- **Old customer details.** If the customer has renamed itself or moved, the act should carry the current data.
- **A signatory without authority.** Someone who is not authorised to sign for the company signs anyway. Ask the customer who signs acts on their side before you send the first one.
- **One act for two contracts.** Work under two different contracts merged into one act makes reconciliation harder for both sides. Keep one act per contract per period.
